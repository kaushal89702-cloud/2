About Us

Built by students, for students.

StudentOS AI began as a simple idea: instead of juggling twenty different apps and tabs for studying, planning, calculating and career prep — why not have one unified operating system for student life?

Every tool on this site runs entirely in your browser. No sign-ups, no data collection, no backend servers — just fast, private, offline-friendly tools that respect your time.
